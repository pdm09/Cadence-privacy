Privacy Policy
Cadence — ADHD Habit Tracker  ·  Last updated: May 2, 2026

Cadence does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Overview
Cadence — ADHD Habit Tracker ("Cadence", "the App", "we") is a personal wellness app designed to help adults with ADHD build sustainable daily habits. We are fully committed to your privacy. This policy explains what data the App uses and how it is handled.

Data We Collect
Cadence collects no personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any identifying information of any kind.

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, using on-device storage (AsyncStorage):

Habits you create (name, category, frequency, color)
Daily habit completion logs
Daily mood check-in selections
Journal entries you write
Daily wins you select
Your streak count
This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server, cloud service, or third party — by us or anyone else.

Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party for any purpose whatsoever.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or tracking SDKs.

Data Storage & Security
All app data is stored using AsyncStorage on your local device. This data is subject to your device's own security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). If you delete the App, all associated data is permanently and irrecoverably removed from your device.

Children's Privacy
Cadence is designed for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this App.

Changes to This Policy
If we ever change this policy in a meaningful way (for example, if we add cloud sync or analytics), we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
