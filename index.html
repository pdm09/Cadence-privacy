🐦‍🔵 Privacy Policy Robin — ADHD Self Care · Last updated: May 1, 2026 Robin does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. Overview Robin — ADHD Self Care ("Robin", "the App", "we") is a personal wellness app designed to support adults with ADHD. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains what data the App uses and how it is handled. Data We Collect Robin collects no personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information. The App stores the following data locally on your device only, using Apple's on-device storage (AsyncStorage): Daily mood check-in selections (stored as a number 0–4) Goals and tasks you create (title, notes, time estimate, priority, completion status) Journal entries you write Daily wins you select Your streak count This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server, cloud service, or third party — by us or anyone else. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party for any purpose. We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. Data Storage & Security All app data is stored using AsyncStorage on your local device. This data is subject to your device's own security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). If you delete the App, all associated data is permanently removed. Children's Privacy Robin is designed for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this App. Changes to This Policy If we ever change this policy in a meaningful way (for example, if we add cloud sync or analytics), we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
